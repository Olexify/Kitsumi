hovered word to the service; nothing is stored by Kitsumi, and the answer is
shown with the service's name on it. Kitsumi is not affiliated with any of
them. The data behind them, where the service states it:

11. Word packs for Advanced scanning
------------------------------------
These are NOT part of this package. In the Dict tab (Advanced scanning) the
user can download a word pack for a language; it is fetched only then, from
Kitsumi's own public repository (raw.githubusercontent.com/Olexify/KitsumiOPS,
folder packs/), checked against a SHA-256 checksum, and kept in that user's
browser. A pack is a list of word forms with a few grammar flags (which verb
or adjective class a word belongs to, whether it is common), extracted from
the dictionary below; it contains no definitions. Being derived from a
CC BY-SA work, each pack is itself shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

  Japanese pack (scan-ja-1)   JMdict, by the Electronic Dictionary Research
                              and Development Group (EDRDG), CC BY-SA 4.0
                              https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html
  Chinese pack (scan-zh-1)    CC-CEDICT, published by MDBG, CC BY-SA 4.0
                              https://cc-cedict.org/wiki/

Licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The scripts that build the packs are in tools/packs/ of the source code.
