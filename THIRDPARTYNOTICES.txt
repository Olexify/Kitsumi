8. Dictionaries offered in the catalog
--------------------------------------
These are NOT part of this package. Kitsumi hosts none of them and ships none
of them. The catalog is a list of links; a file is fetched from a third-party
host only when the user asks for it, and it then lives in that user's own
browser. Each keeps whatever licence its publisher gave it, and the obligation
to honour that licence is the publisher's and the user's, not Kitsumi's.
